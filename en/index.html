04 / ARTIST

About the artist

Sorin Iosub (born in 1975 in Solonț, Bacău County) is a Romanian painter and visual artist. He graduated in Painting from the Faculty of Fine Arts in Timișoara (2005) and completed a master’s degree in Sources and Resources of the Image in Contemporary Painting at the West University of Timișoara (2007). He has been a member of the Union of Visual Artists of Romania since 2007.

Working in painting, watercolor and drawing, he explores metamorphosis, imagination, and the interplay of form and color through surrealist-influenced compositions and animal-inspired figures. His work has been exhibited in Romania, Hungary, Bulgaria and Ecuador. His distinctions include First Prize at the Lascăr Vorel Art Biennial (2007) and the Youth First Prize at the Timișoara Visual Arts Salon (2006).
